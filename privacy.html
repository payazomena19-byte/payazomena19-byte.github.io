Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Fact Arsenal Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to his own YouTube channel.

What data it uses

The app signs in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and related details on the owner's channel. It does not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own device or account so uploads can continue without signing in each time. No data is sent to third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
